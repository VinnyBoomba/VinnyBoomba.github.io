Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Personal Automation Hub describes private automation projects operated for the account owner's own use. This policy covers the projects described here; individual Google Apps Script projects request only their separately authorized permissions.

Information accessed

Depending on the project and the permissions granted, automations may access: (1) YouTube channel, playlist, and video identifiers and metadata, and authorized playlist management functions; (2) Gmail messages, including sender, subject, dates, receipt and renewal details, and relevant message content; (3) Google Sheets data for configuration, payment and subscription records, sports picks, and processing logs; (4) Google Drive files when explicitly configured for that purpose; and (5) execution metadata and error logs. Some projects also access public data sources, including sports information.

How information is used

Information is processed only for the owner's requested personal automation tasks: discovering eligible YouTube videos, maintaining owner-selected playlists, identifying potential subscriptions and billing changes, preparing summaries, keeping spreadsheet records, and preventing duplicate processing. Gmail receipt candidates may require owner review before being treated as confirmed payments. The projects do not use Google user data for targeted advertising, resale, or unrelated profiling.

Storage and security

Project configurations, subscription and payment evidence, processing history, and operational logs may be stored in owner-controlled Google Sheets, Apps Script properties, and associated Google services. Authorization is handled through Google's OAuth mechanisms. This informational website does not collect visitors' Gmail content, Google account credentials, payment card credentials, or OAuth tokens. No system can promise absolute security, and access to the underlying Google resources depends on Google account permissions and sharing settings.

Sharing and disclosure

Personal information is not sold or intentionally shared with advertisers. Data may be processed by Google services and, where an individual script explicitly uses an external API, by the relevant service as needed for that feature. Projects do not intentionally publish private Gmail, Google Sheets, Drive, or YouTube account data on this website.

Retention and deletion

Historical processing records may be retained while a project is in use, including subscription history needed to identify annual renewals, price changes, and cancellations, and playlist history needed to prevent duplicate additions. The owner can remove stored records in their sheets, stop triggers, delete Apps Script projects, and revoke Google authorization. Service-side backups or audit records may remain according to the applicable provider's retention policies.

Owner control

The owner chooses which scripts to run and which Google permissions to grant. Google account access can be reviewed or revoked through Google Account connections. Revoking access may stop related features.

Platform policies

YouTube integrations use YouTube API Services and are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. See Google's Privacy Policy. Information obtained through YouTube API Services is used only for authorized playlist-related functions.

Contact

For questions regarding the personal automations, use the developer support contact provided in the relevant Google OAuth consent screen.
