Application policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Watch Later Auto is a personal-use application operated by its account owner. This policy describes how the automation accesses, uses, stores, and shares Google and YouTube data.

Information accessed

When authorized by the owner, the application may access YouTube channel and playlist identifiers, video identifiers and metadata (such as titles, descriptions, publication times, duration, language, and live status), and information needed to add videos to a playlist. It accesses Google Sheets data used to configure the automation and record its results.

How the information is used

Accessed information is used only to discover candidate videos, apply owner-defined filters, add matching videos to the owner's chosen playlist, avoid duplicate additions, and maintain execution and error logs. The application does not use this information for advertising or profiling.

Storage and security

Rules, pending video IDs, previously processed video IDs, and operational logs are stored in a Google Sheets spreadsheet controlled by the owner. Google's Apps Script and OAuth services handle execution and authorization. The application does not intentionally publish private playlist contents or OAuth tokens on this website.

Sharing

Personal data is not sold or intentionally shared with advertisers or other independent third parties. Google and YouTube services process data to provide the authorized functions of the automation, subject to their own policies.

Retention and deletion

Processing history may be retained indefinitely so videos removed manually are not re-added. The owner can stop scheduled executions, revoke authorization in Google Account settings, and delete the Google Sheet and Apps Script project to remove stored automation data. Some service-side records may remain under Google's retention policies.

User control

The owner controls which channels to monitor, which videos qualify, the destination playlist, and whether the automation remains active. Google authorization may be revoked at any time.

Relevant platform policies

This automation uses the YouTube API Services. See YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

For questions concerning this personal application, use the developer support email shown on its Google authorization consent screen.
