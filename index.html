Personal Automation Hub

This site explains a collection of personal automation projects developed and operated by one account owner. Projects use Google Apps Script and authorized Google APIs to help organize YouTube playlists, track personal subscriptions, generate reports, and manage user-controlled spreadsheets.

This is an informational site, not a hosted public service. Individual automations run in the owner's Google account and may be at different stages of development.

Projects and intended functions

  • Watch Later Auto: Checks selected YouTube channels and playlists, evaluates video metadata against owner-defined rules, and adds eligible videos to an authorized private playlist. Google Sheets maintains rules and processing history.
  • Subscription Tracker: Searches authorized Gmail messages for potential subscription receipts, renewals, price changes, promotions, trials, and cancellations. Stores findings and review decisions in Google Sheets and is intended to send the owner monthly subscription summaries. Candidate transactions are subject to review; ordinary retail receipts are not treated as subscriptions solely because they recur.
  • Sports Picks Tracker: Uses owner-maintained Google Sheets to record sports selections, public odds observations, game results, and analysis. External sports data feeds are separate from Google account data.
  • Personal reports and newsletters: Uses scheduled Apps Script jobs, applicable public data sources, and owner-controlled Google Sheets to compile requested personal summaries.

Google data and permissions

Depending on the specific script authorized, projects may request access to Gmail messages, Google Sheets files, Google Drive files, or YouTube playlists and video metadata. Access is limited by the permissions granted to each script, and not every project uses every service.

Gmail message content may be processed for subscription classification and reporting. Private account data is not displayed on this website. No personal Gmail messages, spreadsheet data, YouTube playlist contents, or access tokens are published here.
