Watch Later Auto is a personal-use automation for organizing YouTube videos into a playlist controlled by the authorized account owner. It is not a public subscription or hosted service.

Availability and video coverage depend on Google services, API quotas, authorization, and access restrictions. Some videos, including members-only videos, may not be discoverable through the API. No uninterrupted operation or complete discovery is guaranteed.

The owner can disable the script's triggers, revoke Google authorization, and delete the associated project and spreadsheet at any time.
